About Us

Traverse Dynamics Global LLC is a Colorado-based construction consulting firm specializing in high-end tiny homes and modular residential and commercial projects utilizing advanced steel framing techniques.

Operating out of our steel Quonset hut HQ, we combine rugged durability with clean, minimal design and modern building technology.

Led by Cydney Skeens, we blend deep industry experience with a DIY manufacturing mindset using Scottsdale Construction Systems roll formers and state-of-the-art design and engineering software. Our mission is to deliver precision, efficiency, and innovation—projects that stand the test of time while reducing cost and environmental impact.

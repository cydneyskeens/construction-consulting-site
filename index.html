Meet Cydney

Steel framing. Roll forming. Manufacturing. Field experience.

Cydney Skeens is a light-gauge steel professional with hands-on experience in roll-forming equipment, steel-frame manufacturing, panel assembly, production workflows, and field operations.

Through Traverse Dynamics Global, Cydney works across the steel-framing process — helping connect design, equipment, manufacturing, logistics, and construction. Her work ranges from individual steel-frame projects to mobile manufacturing and consulting for companies developing their own production capabilities.

She is also the creator of ScotCyd, an educational series that takes viewers behind the scenes of roll forming and light-gauge steel manufacturing.
